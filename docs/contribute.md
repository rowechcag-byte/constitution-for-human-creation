# Get involved

Constitution for Human Creation grows through people who care about how powerful AI fits into human life. If you'd like to contribute, we'd love to hear from you.

## Ways to help

- **Review and comment** on the [Foundational Principles](foundational-principles.md) or the [Constitution](constitution.md).
- **Bring expertise** in law, policy, ethics, engineering, research, education, or community organizing.
- **Host or join a pilot.** See the [pilot sheets](pilot-sheets.md) for the two small pilot shapes.
- **Translate** the texts into other languages.
- **Share it** with people and groups who should be part of the conversation.

## Contact us

Email **[Rowe.heikkinen@chcag.org](mailto:Rowe.heikkinen@chcag.org?subject=Interested%20in%20contributing)** and tell us a little about yourself:

1. Your name and where you're based
2. How you'd like to help
3. Any relevant background or links

We read every message and will get back to you.
